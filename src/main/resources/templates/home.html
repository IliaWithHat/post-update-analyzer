How it works

  1. Before the update: connect the phone, open it here and take a Before update snapshot.
  2. Install the system update on the phone.
  3. After the update: connect the phone again and take an After update snapshot.
  4. Compare them: you see which apps the update installed, reinstalled or turned back on, and can uninstall, disable or restore each one.
